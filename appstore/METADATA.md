## Description

```text
Turn a ProTap card or any NFC tag into a tap-to-share business card.

Protap Club writes your details onto an NFC tag. Anyone who taps it with their phone opens your website, saves your contact or lands on your profile. They don't need the app.

Write to a tag:
• Website or any link
• Contact card with name, phone, email and company
• Social profile on Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Facebook, GitHub, TikTok or YouTube
• Plain text
• Actions: call a number, send a prefilled message, start a FaceTime call, or open an address or map location

Read any tag to see what's stored on it, and reset a tag to wipe it clean and reuse it.

Works with ProTap cards, stickers and accessories, and with standard NDEF NFC tags.

Everything happens on your iPhone. The app has no account and doesn't collect your data.
```

## Review notes

```text
Protap Club reads and writes NFC tags, so it needs a physical iPhone and an NDEF-formatted NFC tag (any NTAG213/215/216 sticker or card works). The Simulator has no NFC and shows "NFC Not Supported" on launch.

To test: tap "Write NFC Tag" → "Write URL", enter a link, tap Write and hold the tag to the top of the iPhone. Then go back, tap "Read NFC Tag" and scan the same tag to see the link. "Reset Tag" erases it again.

No login is required. The app makes no network requests.
```
